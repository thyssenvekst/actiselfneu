Support

If you have questions or a problem with our actiself app, simply write to us at support@biomentric.zohodesk.eu or read the FAQs on this page:

General information:

Which platforms is actiself available for?

Our app is currently only available for iOS (Apple). An Android version is already in preparation.

Which languages is the app available in?

The app is currently available in German only. English and French versions will be available from early 2027. If you are interested, write to us at support@biomentric.zohodesk.eu.

Do I need a smartwatch or fitness tracker to use actiself?

Our app also works without a wearable. However, important features are then unavailable or only available to a limited extent.

actiself records a lot of health data. How do you handle this data?

The data collected is used exclusively for the app’s features and to validate its effectiveness. We take great care to ensure that your data is processed securely and protected from unauthorised access. We guarantee that your data will never be made available to third parties for purposes such as marketing.

Which personal data do I have to provide when signing up?

We need neither your name nor your address, phone number or any other personal data. For communication and sign-up, we only need an email address. To increase your privacy, we recommend using an email address that does not contain your real name. You can choose freely how the app addresses you, including a nickname.

Why does the app need access to location services? Do you track where I am?

Our app shows you how many different places you have visited in recent weeks. We never store your exact location data. Precise tracking is therefore ruled out.
