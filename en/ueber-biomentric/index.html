Our story

The actiself app is the result of years of research and development by scientists at the Max Planck Institute of Psychiatry in Munich.

  1. 2018

    With the support of Max Planck Innovation, the SMART project was launched at the institute in 2018 to research the use of wearables for the diagnosis and therapy of mental disorders.

  2. 2018 – 2024

    During this period, a first study app for smartwatches was developed and tested in various departments of the clinic affiliated with the institute.

  3. 2024

    In 2024, the technology transfer project SMART became the start-up Biomentric, founded by two researchers from the institute with the participation of Max Planck Innovation.

  4. Jan. 2025

    In January 2025, Biomentric launched its first app, actiself.
