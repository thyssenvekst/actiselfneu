The app

Get active for your mental health!

On this page
  1. We support you step by step on your way to better wellbeing – with wearables
  2. How does actiself work?

Chronic, high stress can lead to serious health problems such as burnout or even depression. Lack of exercise is an additional risk factor and also weighs on your mental health. With a programme lasting several weeks, actiself helps you cope better with stress, move more and become more active. This way you can protect yourself from overload and prevent long-term health problems.

We support you step by step on your way to better wellbeing – with wearables

With smartwatches such as an Apple Watch or fitness trackers, actiself makes your progress towards a more active lifestyle visible and helps you reach your personal goals. You decide what you want to achieve. At the same time, monitoring with wearables helps actiself understand where you need support and give you more targeted recommendations.

How does actiself work?

The actiself programme consists of 42 exercises on topics such as stress management, dealing with negative thoughts, sleep hygiene and behavioural activation. All content consists of effective, evidence-based elements from behavioural therapy. With one exercise a day, you can promote your mental health over 6 weeks. With actiself you also practise building more exercise and pleasant and social activities into your daily life. actiself accompanies you with daily questions about your mood and your sleep.
